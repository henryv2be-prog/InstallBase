# Create-video sound loops

Short MP3 loops used when installers build a vertical video from photos.

- Generated in-repo (royalty-free) via `node scripts/generate-compilation-sounds.mjs`.
- You can replace any `.mp3` here with your own **licensed** tracks (same filename) — e.g. downloads from [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) under their content license.
- Do not commit copyrighted commercial music (e.g. film/TikTok trending songs) without proper rights.
